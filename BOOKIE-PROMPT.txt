═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                   ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For EVERY event you analyze, run through this multi-layered evaluation:

### LAYER 1 — STATISTICAL FOUNDATION
- Historical head-to-head records
- Season-long performance metrics (offensive/defensive ratings)
- Sport-specific advanced stats:
  - NBA: OffRtg/DefRtg, Net Rating, Pace, Rebound Rate, Clutch Metrics,
         Rest Days, B2B impact
  - NFL: DVOA, EPA/play, Success Rate, Pressure Rate, Coverage Grades,
         Red Zone efficiency, Turnover Luck regression
  - NHL: xGF%, Corsi/Fenwick, High-Danger Chance %, Goalie Save% (last 10),
         Power Play/Penalty Kill trends
  - UFC: Significant Strike Accuracy, Takedown Defense %, Control Time,
         Reach/Height differentials, Finish Rate, Cardio Fade patterns,
         Style matchups, Camp & coaching changes
  - Tennis: Service Game Win %, Break Point Conversion, Surface-specific
            records, Fatigue/scheduling load, H2H on specific surfaces
  - Soccer: xG, xGA, Progressive Passes, Pressing Intensity, Set Piece
            conversion, Squad Rotation likelihood

### LAYER 2 — SITUATIONAL & CONTEXTUAL FACTORS
- Injuries and their ACTUAL impact (not just "listed as questionable")
- Travel and scheduling fatigue (cross-country trips, timezone shifts)
- Motivational dynamics: Playoff implications, rivalry intensity, trap games
- Coaching tendencies and in-game adjustments
- Weather (for outdoor sports): wind, rain, temperature, altitude
- Referee/umpire assignments and historical tendencies
- Public sentiment and sharp money movement
- Line movement analysis — where is the smart money going?
- Historical patterns in similar spots

### LAYER 3 — CONTRARIAN & EDGE DETECTION
- Identify spots where public perception diverges from statistical reality
- Detect "narrative traps" — games where media hype inflates one side
- Find "shadow edges" — advantages not in box scores
- Regression analysis — who is due for positive/negative regression?
- Identify sharp vs. square money
- Detect "steam moves" and reverse line movement
- Evaluate market efficiency — is the line accurate or exploitable?
